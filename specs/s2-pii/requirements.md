# Slice 2, spaCy pass and PII mask: requirements

Plan §5 stage 3: one spaCy pass (`en_core_web_sm`, already installed) yields noun chunks, noun/adjective/number unigrams and bigrams, intent spans split at conjunctions, named entities for the PII mask, and cues for dates, negation and existence. Users may type client details; names must be masked before any text reaches a model provider (Groq, Cloudflare, Modal). Privacy rule of the project: nothing about a person is stored or sent if it can be avoided.

- R1 `engine/chapterverse/nlp.py`: `analyse(text) -> Analysis` with `noun_chunks`, `terms` (unigrams and bigrams of NOUN, PROPN, ADJ, NUM lemmas), `intent_spans` (text split at coordinating conjunctions between clauses, using the dependency parse), `entities` (text, label, span), `cues` (has_date, has_negation, has_existence_question such as "is there a law", "does ... exist"). Load the model once per process (thread-safe lazy load); protected citation spans (passed in as character spans) are merged into single tokens with `retokenize` so the parser does not split them.
- R2 `engine/chapterverse/pii.py`: `mask_pii(text, analysis) -> Masked` replacing person and organisation names (spaCy labels PERSON, ORG, and FAC when the text ends in Ltd, Limited, LLP, plc, Inc or similar), email addresses, UK phone numbers, UK postcodes and National Insurance numbers (regexes) with stable placeholders `[PERSON 1]`, `[ORG 1]`, `[EMAIL 1]`... The same name gets the same placeholder within one text. Instrument titles and the names in citations are never masked (for example "Employment Rights Act 1996", "Equality Act 2010", "Secretary of State"): skip entities that overlap a protected citation span or match a title in the router index. `Masked` keeps a reverse map only in memory for the request; nothing is logged.
- R3 Latency: p99 of analyse plus mask under 15 ms for a 300-character query on this Mac after warm-up; report model load time and RSS added.
- R4 Tests: "Can my employer Acme Ltd dismiss John Smith for whistleblowing?" masks both and keeps "whistleblowing"; "Equality Act 2010 s.39" untouched; email, phone, postcode and NI number masked; repeated names share a placeholder; intent split on "and" between two questions; negation cue; existence cue.

## Addendum (orchestrator review, 21 Nov 2026)

Probe results on the first version:
- "Can Priya Nkemelu claim redundancy pay from Zentrix Ltd?" masked Zentrix Ltd but NOT "Priya Nkemelu" (spaCy NER missed the name). A missed name reaches the provider; this is the direction that matters.
- "Email me at ... NI AB123456C ..." masked "Email" as [PERSON 1] and "NI" as [ORG 1].

- R5 Recall fallback for names: after NER, mask any run of one or more tokens tagged PROPN (or capitalised and absent from the SymSpell dictionary at `artefacts/symspell/dictionary.txt`, if that file is available) that is not the first token of the sentence, not inside a protected span, not part of an instrument title or citation, not a month or day name, not in a short stoplist of legal and public terms (Act, Regulations, Order, Secretary, State, Crown, Parliament, England, Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland, UK, United Kingdom, HMRC, ACAS, Employment Tribunal, Tribunal, Court, EU). Mask as [NAME n]. When in doubt, mask: privacy over precision. Test: "Priya Nkemelu" masked; "Employment Tribunal" and "Scotland" kept.
- R6 False positives: the first word of a sentence is never a PERSON on its own unless spaCy labels a multi-token PERSON span; "NI" is masked only as part of a National Insurance number match. Test both cases above.
