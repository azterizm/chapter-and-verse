The cited-window judge (S5.1b, policy `STRICT_FIGURES+CITED_JUDGE`) failed the sealed held-out rule: 10 more correct paraphrases were rolled back because the judge saw one subsection without the rest of its section (`reports/heldout-qwen-modal-cited.md`). Engine premises hold about 8 windows from about 6 sections (legal battery dev), so a judge that sees every window of the cited SECTIONS keeps the section context and still cuts most of the judge prompt.

- R1 Engine: a third scope in `engine/chapterverse/audit/verify.py`: kept candidates are those whose first window's SECTION is the section of any cited window. Section of a coordinate: the instrument coordinate plus the first provision part (`s12`, `reg4`, `art5`, ...); for a schedule, `schN` plus the next part (`sch1/para2`); a coordinate with no provision part is its own section. Reuse one helper for this (there is a `_section_coordinate` in `engine/chapterverse/discovery.py`; move it to a shared place both can import, e.g. `chapterverse/citations.py` or a small new module, without changing its behaviour; discovery tests must stay green). Windows with no coordinate: kept only when a cited window also has none (same rule as today's cited scope otherwise). All other R1 acceptance criteria of `specs/s5-judge-cited/requirements.md` apply unchanged (claim checks on the full premise, the chosen window is always a kept one, fallback when nothing is cited, judge numbering 1..k).
