# Private documents and bare sections: requirements

Live test, 21 Nov 2026, with `PROBE-CLAUSE-UK-001` from `~/Code/rag-security-probes`: "Under Section 4 of the Mayfair Lease Agreement, who bears the cost of routine maintenance and filter replacements for internal HVAC units?" The router returns `ROUTE_AMBIGUOUS` with reason `provision_without_instrument` (it reads "Section 4" but no Act), and the app offered s.4 of five unrelated Acts (Companies Act 2006, ERA 1996, Equality Act 2010, Insolvency Act 1986, TULRCA 1992). Nothing unsafe happened, but the options were noise and the real situation (a private document this app cannot read) was not stated.

- R1 Private documents: when routing gives `provision_without_instrument` (or `ROUTE_UNRESOLVED`) AND the question names a non-legislation document, answer with a `RefusalEvent` of kind `out_of_scope` and the message: "Chapter & Verse covers UK legislation only. It cannot read private documents such as leases, contracts or policies. Ask about the law that applies, or name an Act or SI and a section." Detect a named private document with a deterministic rule: a capitalised name followed by one of Agreement, Lease, Contract, Policy, Handbook, Deed, Licence, License, Terms, Conditions, Terms and Conditions, Constitution, Articles of Association, Memorandum, Bylaws, Rules, Code of Conduct, Manual, Plan, Schedule, Letter, Notice, Will, Trust Deed, Statement; or the phrases "my lease", "my contract", "our policy", "the agreement", "the attached", "uploaded", "this document". Instrument titles that the router knows (Acts, SIs, Orders, Regulations, Rules made under an Act) are never matched (check the router index title lookup, as `pii.py` does).
- R2 Bare section without a document: when routing gives `provision_without_instrument` and R1 does not apply, do not show the router's list of the same section in unrelated instruments. Run discovery on the question with the bare section reference removed and show those candidates in the clarify event with the question "Which Act or SI do you mean? These provisions may be related." If discovery finds nothing, ask "Which Act or SI do you mean?" with no candidates.
- R3 Tests (fixture router and store): the Mayfair question gives the out_of_scope refusal with no provider call; "What does section 4 of my lease say about repairs?" the same; "What does section 4 say about the limit of the compensatory award?" gives discovery candidates, not unrelated s.4 candidates; "section 124 of the Employment Rights Act 1996" still binds; "Rules" in "Civil Procedure Rules 1998" style titles is not treated as a private document when the router knows the title.

## Addendum (orchestrator review)
False refusal found: "Can my employer change my contract without my consent?" is refused because "my contract" matches anywhere. That is a question about the law, not about the content of a private document.
- R4 Refuse as a private document only when the question asks for the CONTENT of a private document:
  - a named private document with a capitalised name (for example "the Mayfair Lease Agreement", "the AcmeCloud contract", "the ZenithHost SaaS Agreement"), or
  - a content request about one: "what does (my|our|the|this) (contract|lease|policy|agreement|handbook|document|warranty|terms) say", "under (clause|section|paragraph) N of (my|our|the) ...", "according to (my|our|the) ...", "summarise/summarize (the|this) (uploaded|attached) ...", "uploaded", "attached document", "in our (policy|contract|agreement|handbook|records|repositories|vector stores)".
  - Bare mentions ("change my contract", "end my tenancy agreement", "my employment contract", "a settlement agreement") are NOT refused.
- R5 Tests: refused: the Mayfair question; "What does our policy say about remote working?"; "Summarise the uploaded document."; "Under clause 4 of my lease, who pays for repairs?". Not refused: "Can my employer change my contract without my consent?"; "What notice must my landlord give to end my tenancy agreement?"; "Is a settlement agreement binding if I did not get legal advice?"; "What is the minimum notice period under my employment contract?"; "Does the Highway Code apply to cyclists?"; "What does the Civil Procedure Rules 1998 say about disclosure?".
