# S3.4b Do not "correct" gibberish or short words into unrelated words: requirements

Live on 22 and 23 Nov 2026: the query "zxqv blorptastic wibbleflange" gets the notice `Spelling corrected: "zxqv" read as "xiv"` and section chips, where the right outcome is the negative-evidence receipt ("No provision matched"). Cause: `engine/chapterverse/normalise.py` `_correct_word` corrects every unprotected word longer than `MIN_CORRECTION_WORD_LEN` (3) with `max_edit_distance=2`, so a 4-letter string two edits from a dictionary word is rewritten.

- R1 Length-scaled edit distance: allowed edit distance for a correction is 0 for words of 3 letters or fewer (as now), 1 for 4 to 7 letters, 2 for 8 letters or more (SymSpell distance is Damerau OSA, so a swap of two letters is 1). Apply it to the lookup and to the chosen suggestion.
- R2 Not a word: never correct a token that has no vowel (a, e, i, o, u, y) or that contains a run of 4 or more consonants other than common English clusters you can justify (keep the rule simple; say what you chose), or that has digits.
- R3 Keep real corrections: these must still be corrected exactly as now (add tests): "unfiar dismisal" to "unfair dismissal"; "compensaton" to "compensation"; "tribunel" to "tribunal"; "landlrod" to "landlord"; "employmnet" to "employment"; "discrimnation" to "discrimination". If any of them is not corrected today, say so and do not force it.
