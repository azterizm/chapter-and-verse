Sprint 4 built `batteries/legal/legal.jsonl` (L01 to L40, 20 dev and 20 test, test sealed) and `eval/legal_gate.py` (retrieval gate, answer rubric, recorder). We now grow it to 80 rows with SIs, carve-outs, quotes, multi-instrument and "not in the Act" questions, and make the answer level a regular, stored run.

- R1 Add rows L41 to L80 from `rows.md` in this spec dir to `batteries/legal/legal.jsonl` (keep L01 to L40 byte for byte). Resolve each label to the store coordinate (SIs use `reg`; use the router and `store.instrument` / `store.get` as the Sprint 4 worker did; its scripts are in `$CV_SCRATCH/../5a8af36a-333e-4abd-aae1-107eda07f631/scratchpad/s4-legal-battery/` if that path exists, read only, copy what you need into your scratch). For each row confirm the gold provision exists in the store, is in force (check the store's temporal status for the snapshot), and its heading or text answers the question. For "none" rows confirm the instrument does NOT contain the answer (search its text for the forbidden phrases and the topic) and that the instrument resolves. If a gold looks wrong, do NOT change it: keep the row with `"note": "CHECK: <evidence>"` and list it in your summary. Also check L01 to L40 for provisions no longer in force in the store snapshot (for example Housing Act 1988 s.21 after the Renters' Rights Act 2025) and report them; do not edit those rows.
- R2 Schema additions (backward compatible): `other_instruments` (list of coordinates, default absent or empty): `must_reach`, `should_reach` and citation items may be inside `instrument` or any of these. `answer_checks.expect_abstention` (bool, default false): the answer passes this check only when at least one sentence is the abstention text and no sentence other than abstentions carries a citation to a provision inside the row's instruments. Row `forbidden_phrases` are added to the default list (not replacing it). A row whose `must_reach` is empty passes the retrieval level when the route binds the row's instrument (report `bound: yes/no`) or refuses or clarifies; it never fails retrieval for having no windows.
