# Slice 2, discovery and negative evidence: requirements

Questions that cite nothing route `ROUTE_UNRESOLVED`. Today the app refuses them. The router's `Router.discover(query)` ranks candidate provisions from the concept index (read `~/Code/legal-rag-router/docs/discovery.md` and `docs/contract.md` §5). Contract rule, kept on purpose: never bind or retrieve from a candidate nobody confirmed; `confident` is only a hint (on the sealed test 30% of US-law queries were still "confident"). Auto-binding is deferred to Slice 3's ColBERT gate.

- R1 `engine/chapterverse/discovery.py`: `discover_step(router, query, *, limit=8) -> DiscoveryOutcome`, one of: `Suggest(candidates, confident, question)` or `NoEvidence(receipt)`.
- R2 `Suggest`: build a contract `ClarifyEvent` whose candidates are the discovered coordinates with readable labels (use `chapterverse.citations` label helpers so a candidate reads like "s.98 Employment Rights Act 1996: Fairness"), best first, at most 8; question "Did you mean one of these provisions?" when confident, "These provisions may be related. Choose one, or name the Act and section." otherwise. Clicking a candidate sends its coordinate, which routes `ROUTE_BOUNDED`, as clarify does today.
- R3 `NoEvidence` (no candidates, or reason `no_match` or `no_terms`): a negative-evidence receipt: a `RefusalEvent` of kind `ROUTE_UNRESOLVED` whose message states what was searched ("Searched N provisions in the statute book as of <snapshot> for: <query terms>. No provision matched.") and the snapshot; the terms are the discovery's normalised terms, never the raw query.
- R4 Out of scope cue: if the query is about case law, non-UK law or personal advice (simple rule list for now: words like "case law", "judgment", "precedent", "US", "American", "California", "France"; "should I", "my lawyer"), return a `RefusalEvent` of kind `out_of_scope` before discovery. This is the Slice 2 rule baseline; Slice 6 replaces it with a classifier.
- R5 Latency: report p50 and p99 of `discover_step` over the held-out concept battery queries on the real data.
- R6 Tests on the fixture router and concept index (`tests/conftest.py`): suggestion path, no-evidence path, out-of-scope path, labels, ordering, limit.
